FUNCTIONAL SCOPE
Personal unsecured, vehicle/other credit transaction, ordinary home mortgage; performing natural-person planning accounts only.
Date-driven actual/365 simple interest at constant nominal annual rate. Dated extras first settle interest, fees, capital. Extras do not replace scheduled instalments. First due date 1–62 days after principal/balance date. Month-end anchoring and leap dates handled.
Monthly PMT or entered actual instalment. Final payment explicitly clears residual and balloon; may be larger than ordinary instalments. No negative balances; excess offered funds shown as unused, not debited.
Service/initiation VAT-inclusive inputs, upfront/financed initiation (not double counted), insurance fixed/reducing/none, separate dated documented charges. Monthly service/insurance billed on scheduled dates; no first-period proration or automatic unbilled-settlement proration.
Settlement AFTER same-day payments. Existing-loan original-large checkbox is important: current balance is not necessarily original principal. Fixed large total withheld without supplied lender charge; variable large termination is a three-calendar-month remaining-notice interest approximation, not an exact statutory/lender quote.
No universal NCA maximum principal. Software amount/term caps are numerical guardrails.

WHAT IS NOT COVERED / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED
This package is NOT certification of all SA loan contracts or current laws. Reference initiation/service/insurance/repo screens require qualified review of category, contract date, proportional fee limit, applicable VAT, first-period fees and any amendments. Reference amounts were carried forward from earlier research, not freshly certified here.
Default amounts are manually documented inputs; no collection tariff, section 103(5) cap reconstruction, default history audit, debt review or court costs model. No enhanced/joint-life insurance or affordable-housing compliance assessment, juristic-person NCA applicability, future rate changes, conveyancing or bond cancellation attorney fees.
Fixed insurance cost screen at initial balance is not certification of continuing cover/cost suitability. Obtain actual policy and lender quote.
No daily cents rounding or interest-on-interest compounding. Explain differences from bank statements.

FREE VERSUS PRODUCTION
Render Free sleeps after 15 idle minutes, may take about a minute to wake, can restart, has quotas and ephemeral local storage; Render explicitly says not to use Free instances for production. Use Free to test, then choose an appropriate always-on paid compute instance for a client-facing public service. Upgrading a workspace plan alone does not change the service compute tier.
This app has no database or background worker. Reports are produced in memory and sent synchronously using HTTPS. In-memory rate limits reset on restart; one worker only. Built-in attempt caps: 3/recipient/hour and 40/process-day. Provider quotas still apply. There is no durable auto-retry queue, delivery webhook or guaranteed availability. For service commitments/volume, add shared throttling, durable jobs/status/retries, authenticated delivery webhooks and operational monitoring.
Do not use a free expiring database for durable client records. No database is required by this package.
Application does not persist personal data/PDFs in files; providers may retain messages/logs. Manage retention and security settings with vendors.
